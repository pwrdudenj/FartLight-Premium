First, print out the answer key, print out Q Point cards, then gather up some people and have someone be the "Quiz Master."

First the Quiz Master chooses a random question out of the 30 onscreen.

He, she, or they read the question.

Anyone playing may ding in by saying "Ding!" and try to answer.

Some questions, you don't need to ding in, so keep an eye for that.

If you get the answer right, the Quiz Master will type "DONE" into the input and show how many Q Points the question was worth.

If nobody gets it right for a long time, nobody gets points and the answer is revealed.

Whoever got it right chooses the next question.

After all 30 questions are done, whoever has the most Q Points wins!
